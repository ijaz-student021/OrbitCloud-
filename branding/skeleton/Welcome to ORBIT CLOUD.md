# Welcome to ORBIT CLOUD

This is your personal storage space in **ORBIT CLOUD**, a private cloud.

## What you can do

- **Add** files: drag and drop them here, or press **+ New**.
- **Update** a file: upload a new copy with the same name. Older versions are kept.
- **Download** any file to your computer or phone.
- **Delete** files you no longer need. They wait in *Deleted files* until you empty the trash.

You can reach this cloud from home, the office or your phone. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
